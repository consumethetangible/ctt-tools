⚠ Reminder — Set a Manual Excerpt

After pasting the metadata bar, open the Post panel in the WordPress editor sidebar and paste your opening sentence or two into the Excerpt field. If you skip this, WordPress will auto-generate the excerpt from the post content — which includes the metadata bar values — and they'll bleed into the homepage cards and social shares.
