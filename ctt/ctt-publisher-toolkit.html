Outfit Flagged — see note
Outfit is listed in earlier project notes as a fourth typeface for section labels. The July 2026 audit found section labels (e.g. "NOW PLAYING," metadata bar labels) actually rendering in Josefin Sans, and no Outfit references turned up in the live Additional CSS. Worth a quick DOM check next time you're in Chrome to confirm whether Outfit is used anywhere on the live site, or whether that note is just stale.

Plain-English breakdown of what's actually in ctt-additional-css-current.css, section by section, so you don't have to read raw CSS to know what does what. Click any row to expand.
Hero Header .post-hero-header▶
Sets the hero's minimum height and puts a dark terracotta-brown scrim (86% opacity) over the featured image so white title text stays readable regardless of the photo underneath.
Hero Columns Layout .wp-block-columns▶
Adds the padding around the two-column row inside the hero (title/tags on the left, floating art on the right) and makes sure that row sits above the dark overlay, not behind it.
Hero Left Column .wp-block-post-title / .wp-block-post-terms▶
Styles the title (white, large, heavy drop-shadow so it reads over any photo) and turns each category/tag into a translucent dark pill with light text — and hides WordPress's default "·" separator between them since the pills already look separated.
Hero Right Column .hero-album-art-column▶
Locks the floating album art to a fixed size, gives it a soft terracotta-tinted glow plus a heavier drop shadow, and pulls it up and down with negative margins so it visually "floats" past the top and bottom edge of the hero.
Category & Page Header Images body.category-*▶
One rule sets the default homepage header photo; a separate rule per category (Vinyl, CD, Cassette, Playlists, Miscellany) and per page (Contact, Index of Bands) swaps in a different background photo for that specific archive or page, each with its own position tweak.
Page Title — Default Pages .page-template-default▶
Gives plain pages like Index of Bands and Contact a small terracotta uppercase title with a hairline rule trailing off to the right — the same "section label" look used in the sidebar.
Text Shadows header .wp-block-site-title / .wp-block-navigation▶
A subtle dark shadow behind the site title and nav links so they stay legible over the homepage's photo banner.
Sidebar H3 Headings h3.wp-block-heading▶
Turns every sidebar widget heading ("Now Playing," "Calendar," etc.) into the label-with-trailing-rule treatment, and forces them left-aligned even though some were centered by default.
Embed Styles iframe[src*="youtube/bandcamp"]▶
Adds a drop shadow to embedded YouTube/Bandcamp players (and rounds Bandcamp's corners), plus rounds and shadows regular in-body images so everything in the review body feels consistently "framed."
Post Title Link Colors .entry-title a▶
Post titles are near-black with a terracotta hover everywhere except on dark backgrounds (the hero, category headers), where they flip to white with a light-terracotta hover so they stay readable.
Album Art Hover Excerpt .wp-block-post-featured-image▶
On archive grid thumbnails, hovering the album art fades in a dark overlay with the post excerpt centered on top of it — a little sneak-peek without leaving the grid.
Sidebar Browse Widget .browse-widget / .browse-btn▶
Lays out the decade filter buttons in a 4-column grid and genre buttons in a 2-column grid, each styled as a small pill button that fills solid terracotta on hover.
Single Post — Remove Top Gap .wp-singular .wp-site-blocks▶
Zeroes out theme padding above the content on single posts so the hero sits flush at the top of the page (this is separate from the compact-header gap you're intentionally keeping).
Post Metadata Bar .post-meta-bar▶
The Label/Released/Format/Reviewed strip that sits below the hero on newer reviews — cream background, terracotta left border, values in charcoal, labels in small terracotta caps.
Review Body .entry-content▶
Caps the reading column at 700px, widens the gap between side-by-side columns, blows up the first letter of the opening paragraph into a terracotta Fraunces drop cap, and styles any blockquote with an italic serif treatment and terracotta left border.
Rating Block .post-rating-block▶
The score-and-descriptor strip at the end of a review — large light-weight terracotta numeral on the left, small "Score" label and italic Fraunces descriptor on the right, framed by hairline rules above and below.
Playlist Template Overrides body.post-template-playlists-posts▶
The only two things that change on a Playlist post: the reading column widens to 800px, and the floating hero art becomes a 420×280 rectangle instead of a 300×300 square.
Header Nav — Stick to Bottom header.wp-block-template-part▶
Pins the site title/nav row to the bottom of the header block so it stays anchored there even as the header's photo banner height changes across the homepage vs. category pages.
How the three post types you write for actually differ, structurally — not just in tone.
Reviews
Crown Lands: Apocalypse
CTT Review — the full treatment. Hero with square 300×300 floating art, tag row includes the rating descriptor, metadata bar (Label/Released/Format/Reviewed) below the hero, 700px reading column, Fraunces drop cap, italic blockquote available, rating block at the end.
  • Metadata bar: yes
  • Rating block: yes
  • Reading column: 700px
Playlists
(Un)Focused Definition Vol. 42
CTT Playlist — streamlined. Same hero structure as a Review, but the floating art becomes a wider 420×280 rectangle (built with the Playlist Banner tool) and the reading column widens to 800px. No metadata bar, no rating block — the post is usually just the intro copy plus embedded tracks/banner.
  • Metadata bar: no
  • Rating block: no
  • Reading column: 800px
The 9C Files
Nine Circles: Contributor Review
Nine Circles — a different site entirely. This isn't a CTT template at all — it's a separate WordPress site with its own ice-blue tinted visual identity and its own generative "void engine" ribbon graphics for header art. None of CTT's hero/metadata-bar/rating-block system applies here; formatting follows 9C's house style instead.
  • Shares CTT's design system: no
  • Header art: void-engine ribbon generator (Second Circle Header Creator / Ov Grid Creator)
